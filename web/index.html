🔬 Physical & Literature Interpretation

1. Unruh (1981) & Weinfurtner (2011) Analogy

When fluid speed $U(x)$ exceeds local shallow wave velocity $c(x) = \sqrt{g h(x)}$, a sonic horizon ($\text{Fr} \ge 1$) forms over the obstacle. Upstream waves cannot penetrate the horizon.

2. Residual vs. Naive CNN — Architecture Bias (Not Topology)

Both models receive 2-channel input [wave, eff_vel] and must generalize over varied flow profiles (U₀ and bump randomized per sample). ResConv1D outperforms CNN via GroupNorm + GELU + residual skip connections — not any topological structure. The source of the gap is an ablation hypothesis: smoother gradient flow (residuals) and better cross-sample normalization (GroupNorm). L2-Norm Drift measures numerical fidelity against a dissipative reference — not Hamiltonian conservation.

3. Crowther et al. (2019) + Mandatory Negative Control

Kinematic mathematical equivalence ≠ gravitational physics identity. This 1D toy model simulates a decoupled scalar field, not true nonlinear shallow-water dynamics. Critical: ResConv1D advantage is ... without a horizon vs ... with one — meaning the architecture excels at generic smooth-field interpolation, with or without Fr=1.
